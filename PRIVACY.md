# Samarkand — Privacy Policy

*Effective: August 18, 2026*

Canonical, hosted copy: <https://onyx-systems.ch/privacy/samarkand>. This file is
the source of truth version-controlled alongside the game; the hosted page should
match it. Update both together when the game's data handling changes.

## The short version

Samarkand has no account system and no server. Your progress — souls
collected, unlocked mutations, settings — is saved locally, in your browser
or on your Android device, and never transmitted anywhere.

## What Samarkand stores, and where

- **Run and meta-progression** (souls, unlocked mutations, best runs) — saved
  in your browser's `localStorage` on Web, or in local app storage on Android.
- **Settings** — language, volume, and other preferences — stored the same way.

## No analytics, no accounts

Samarkand does not use analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs of any
kind, and has no account or sign-in system. There is nothing to collect,
because nothing about your play is ever sent anywhere.

## In-game statistics

The live combat statistics and end-of-run summary (damage dealt, DPS, rooms
cleared, and similar figures) are calculated and shown entirely on your own
device, for your own reference. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data for Samarkand (Web) or uninstalling the app
(Android) removes all locally-stored progress. This is permanent and cannot
be undone, since there is no server copy to restore from.

## Children's privacy

Samarkand does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect personal information from
anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change with it.
